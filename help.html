Welcome to The Pickems Club

Test your NFL knowledge, compete against friends, and climb the weekly league standings!

Welcome to The Pick'ems Club — your premier destination for weekly NFL game predictions. Each week during the regular season and playoffs, members lock in their picks for every match, earn points based on correct predictions, and track their performance on the global leaderboard.

Whether you are playing for weekly bragging rights or competing for the overall season championship, make sure to review the scoring system and guidelines below before lock-in time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: When do weekly picks lock?
Picks lock individually for each game right at kickoff. You can modify picks for later games (e.g., Sunday or Monday night games) as long as those specific games have not started yet.
Q: How does weekly scoring work?
You earn 1 point for every correct straight-up game winner predicted. If your league uses confidence points or spread picks, custom point multipliers will be clearly displayed on your weekly pick sheet.
Q: What happens if a game ends in a tie or is canceled?
In the event of a tied game (after overtime), all picks for that game are declared void and 0 points are awarded. Canceled or postponed games moved past the current NFL week will also be excluded from scoring.
Q: What happens if I forget to submit my picks?
Missed games receive 0 points automatically. System default picks or auto-picks are not enabled unless explicitly set in your specific league's custom settings.
Q: How are tiebreakers calculated for weekly leaders?
The weekly tiebreaker requires predicting the total combined score for the Monday Night Football game. The player whose tiebreaker prediction is closest to the actual combined total wins the tiebreaker.

Official League Rules

  1. One Account Per Person: Players may only operate a single account. Creating multiple accounts to test different pick combinations is strictly prohibited and will result in disqualification.
  2. Strict Lock-in Deadlines: All picks must be submitted via the platform before the scheduled kickoff time. Late submissions via email, message, or post-kickoff edits will not be accepted under any circumstances.
  3. Fair Play & Sportsmanship: Respect fellow league members in all commentary and league chat areas. Harassment, excessive trash-talk, or malicious behavior will result in a ban from chat and potential removal from the club.
  4. Final Score Authority: Game results and official scores are verified through official NFL stats. In the event of post-game stat corrections, leaderboard adjustments will be finalized by Tuesday morning following each slate.
