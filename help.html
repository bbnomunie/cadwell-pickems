Help & Official Rules

Everything you need to know before you lock in your picks — scoring, tiebreakers, rankings, and the ground rules for the league.

Welcome to The Pickems Club — pick the winner of every NFL game each week, earn credit for the ones you get right, and climb the league standings against your friends and coworkers. Whether you're playing for weekly bragging rights or the season-long crown, take a minute to read through the FAQ and rules below before your first pick locks in.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: When do weekly picks lock?

Picks lock at a single time the Commissioner sets for the whole week — not separately for each game. If no lock time is set, picks lock automatically at the first kickoff of the week. You can change your picks and your tiebreaker prediction as many times as you like before then.

Q: How does weekly scoring work?

It's straight-up winner picks — you get credit for every game where your pick matches the official final winner. There are no confidence points, spread picks, or multipliers; every correct pick counts the same.

Q: What happens if a game ends in a tie, or is canceled or postponed?

The game is closed out as having no winner. Nobody gets credit for their pick on that game either way — it's excluded entirely from both the weekly and season standings, for everyone. If a final score exists (as with a tie), it's still shown on the game card even though the game doesn't count toward anyone's record.

Q: What happens if I miss submitting a pick for a game?

That game simply won't count toward your record, win or lose — the same as a voided game. There's no auto-pick feature, so a game you didn't pick is just skipped rather than scored as a loss.

Q: How is the weekly tiebreaker calculated?

Each week, predict the combined total points scored by both teams in the Monday Night game. If two or more players end the week with the same number of correct picks, whoever's prediction is closest to the actual combined score ranks higher for that week.

Q: How do leaderboard rankings and ties work?

Rankings use Standard Competition Ranking — the same method used across most professional sports — so ties are handled fairly on both leaderboards.

Season standings are ranked purely by total correct picks. If two players are tied on correct picks, they share the same rank.

Weekly standings work the same way, except the Monday Night tiebreaker prediction breaks a tie first — two players only share a weekly rank if they're tied on both correct picks and tiebreaker closeness.

Why ranks skip: when players share a rank, the next rank down accounts for all of them. For example:

  • Player A: 14 correct picks → 1st Place
  • Player B: 14 correct picks → 1st Place (tied)
  • Player C: 13 correct picks → 3rd Place (2nd is skipped — two players already hold 1st)
  • Player D: 12 correct picks → 4th Place

This way your rank always reflects exactly how many people are genuinely ahead of you.

Official League Rules

  1. 1

    One Account Per Person — each player may operate a single account. Creating multiple accounts to test different pick combinations is not allowed and may result in disqualification.

  2. 2

    Strict Lock-in Deadlines — all picks must be submitted through the site before the week's lock time. Late submissions by text, chat, or any other channel won't be accepted under any circumstances.

  3. 3

    Fair Play & Sportsmanship — respect fellow league members in chat and everywhere else. Harassment or malicious behavior can result in a chat ban or removal from the club.

  4. 4

    Results Are Automatic, Never Manual — every game result comes from official NFL data pulled in automatically; nobody, including the Commissioner, can manually type in a score or declare a winner. Once a game is graded, that result is final. A tied, canceled, or postponed game is closed out by the Commissioner as having no winner — never as a win for either team.
